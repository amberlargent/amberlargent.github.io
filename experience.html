Process backlog audit

When a process backlog grew and ownership was unclear, I created an audit tracker to document each item, last-touch date, current status, and next step. The audit surfaced that the larger issue was a broken accountability model, not individual effort alone.

That visibility helped managers improve ownership, adjust the process, and create a recurring weekly update rhythm. The work later moved into a more efficient system and remains in use today.

Audit design Accountability Weekly rhythm Process improvement

AI Enablement

Daily AI workflow design, not just AI interest

I use AI tools daily and am known as an early adopter, resource sharer, prompt helper, presenter, bug finder, and practical problem-solver. My focus is helping people use AI in ways that are useful, repeatable, and grounded in the work.

Portable chief-of-staff workflow

Built and use a portable AI workflow model that uses an internal enterprise assistant with a structured Obsidian knowledge vault. Instead of locking agents, skills, prompts, contacts, project context, and routing notes inside one tool, I keep reusable logic in a structured vault that can support multiple AI tools based on the task.

This approach supports context reuse, faster setup, cleaner handoffs between tools, just-in-time data routing, and lower-friction AI-assisted work.
