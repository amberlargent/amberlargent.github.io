Reporting & operational analysis

I use operational, quality, survey, audit, case, and call-review data to identify performance patterns and process gaps.

Reports for leaders

I created quarterly and ad hoc readouts for managers, senior managers, area managers, business managers, and one VP-level audience. The reports covered team trends, coaching opportunities, process recommendations, project status, risks, and follow-up.

My tools have included Numbers, Excel, Wrike dashboards and reports, Qualtrics analytics, Tableau, Keynote, internal dashboards, case and call-review tools, quality forms, and audit trackers.

Process backlog audit

When a backlog grew and ownership was unclear, I created a tracker recording each item, its last-touch date, status, and next step. The audit exposed a broken accountability model. Managers used the findings to improve ownership and the process, with a recurring weekly update. The work later moved into a more efficient system and remains in use.

Coaching & performance support

As a Senior Specialist, I used observation, call evaluation, interviews, analytics, audits, and process review to understand performance problems and develop coaching recommendations.

Performance patterns

During one immersion, I found that faulty assumptions about difficult customer interactions contributed to performance problems. I used the data to address those assumptions and support different approaches to hard calls. We considered average handling time alongside other customer-experience measures. The approach helped improve customer experience indicators, including customer satisfaction.

Practice for difficult conversations

I used Box Notes to create interactive practice for communicating difficult “no” decisions to customers. Teams could try language, receive feedback, and develop consistency before using the approach in customer conversations.

Leadership Academy

My development included four rounds of Leadership Academy, a Team Manager career development opportunity focused on coaching, prioritization, and daily team support. My performance-support work has included observation, call evaluation, analytics deep dives, feedback, and mentorship.

Coaching across roles

Across Trainer, Senior Specialist, and Team Manager backfill work, I have supported thousands of people through training, facilitation, mentoring, and performance support. That includes learners, peers, specialists, managers, and area leaders.
