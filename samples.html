Academic coursework

Confident, Compassionate, & Clear Boundaries

I developed this hybrid course during my master’s program to help customer-service professionals communicate limits and unwelcome news. Repair and refund scenarios give learners a setting in which to practice.

The seven lessons address boundaries, questioning, presenting options, delivery, language choices, and closing an interaction. The design includes two formative check-ins, a planned 90-minute live session, and a final assessment.

Tri-Parish website

I planned and launched a custom website serving three parishes. My responsibilities included content strategy, information architecture, design direction, stakeholder communication, GitHub Pages and domain setup, and launch review. I used AI to assist development.

The site replaced recurring website spending, saving the church several hundred dollars per year.

Outside work
