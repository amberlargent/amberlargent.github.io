Outside the Role

The same strengths show up outside of work.

This is the human side of the portfolio: service, follow-through, curiosity, and a willingness to do the necessary work.

Outside work, I am a widowed mom of three, part of a two-kitten family, a Chiefs fan, and a bibliophile. I care about people deeply, but I am also practical, grounded, and hands-on.

After losing my husband, who was a mechanic, I learned how to do my own car maintenance as a way to honor him and keep moving forward. That experience says a lot about how I work: when something needs to be figured out, I am willing to learn, get close to the problem, and do the work.

The same thread runs through my professional work: see the need, learn the system, build a useful path, and follow through.
