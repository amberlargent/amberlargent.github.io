Escalation audit program

I designed and led an audit of ER and engineering escalations. The work drove closure of more than 500 tasks.

Audit scopeMy workWorkflow changes

Audit scope

The backlog lacked reliable information about eligibility, documentation, routing, ownership, and status. The audit established what each record required and who owned the next action.

My work

I created the audit structure, formed and trained the team, and reported systemic gaps to leaders. The findings exposed accountability problems that extended beyond individual records.

Workflow changes

Alongside closing the backlog, the work strengthened policy and workflow controls. I helped define requirements for automated assignment tracking so that the audit findings could inform how future work was assigned.
