Selected projects

Project management

Personalized Learning Journey pilot

I proposed a manager-supported approach to targeted learning and led the global pilot. I selected the quiz questions, mapped them to micro-courses, and wrote the process managers used to make assignments.

The approach reduced training time while improving handling time, resolution rates, and handoffs. It was adopted after the pilot.

Technical operations · Stakeholder testing

Capacity-planning platform

I built a functional prototype in under two weeks after gathering requirements across four manager organizations. It replaces custom planning spreadsheets with a workspace where managers can test assignments and save plans before committing changes to connected systems.

The platform supports workload rebalancing and staffing decisions. Its scope has expanded across the organization, and it is now in stakeholder testing.

Instructional design

I created an asynchronous series covering Chorus, Wrike, Excel/Numbers, and Keynote. I combined existing instruction with role-specific videos and activities that required learners to build usable work for manager review.

How I work

I listen first.Then I build a path forward.

I ask questions before choosing an approach, especially when a process is asking too much of the people using it. I want to understand who owns the work, where it is getting stuck, and what needs to change. I also plan for the person who will maintain it after the handoff.

Systems should work for people.

That means considering people’s time, knowledge, capacity, and working conditions when making decisions.

About me

Outside work, I am a widowed mom of three, a Chiefs fan, a bibliophile, and the current target of the kitten distribution system, which recently delivered a destruction of cats named Samwise and Rosie.

I am also the person who will learn the thing that needs doing. After losing my husband, who was a mechanic, I learned how to do my own car maintenance as a way to honor him, take care of my family, and keep moving forward.
