About

Listen first. Structure second. Move forward together.

I work at the intersection of learning, facilitation, project and process support, data-driven coaching, AI enablement, knowledge systems, and operational visibility.

The throughline is simple: I help people understand what is really happening, create usable structure, and move from symptoms to practical next steps.

That means I can support more than one type of role. For PM work, I bring planning, tracking, follow-through, decision visibility, and stakeholder alignment. For coaching and enablement work, I bring observation, call evaluation, analytics deep dives, feedback, mentorship, facilitation, and practical team support.
